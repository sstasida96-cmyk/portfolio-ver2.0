Overview

A sales-focused landing page built for a real estate agency targeting foreign nationals interested in purchasing properties in Japan. With a modest 1-month campaign window, the goal was to efficiently capture qualified interest from overseas buyers and convert them into clients.

Total ad spend was CAD $379 (CAD $30/day over 1 month), making efficient lead capture essential from day one.

Results

  • Daily budget of CAD $30 over 1 month (CAD $379 total ad spend).
  • Achieved a 14.51% Click-Through Rate (CTR) — significantly above industry average.
  • Generated 7 leads at a Cost Per Lead (CPL) of CAD $54.
  • Converted 3 qualified leads and secured 1 client contract with a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of CAD $379.
  • Closed a deal valued at approximately CAD $4,310.
