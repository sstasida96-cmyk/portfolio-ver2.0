Overview

A new landing page and Google Ads campaign built entirely from the ground up for a satellite office service targeting companies looking to employ disabled workers. The challenge was introducing a niche product to a market unfamiliar with this service model.

With a lean budget of just CAD $13/day over 6 months (CAD $2,328 total), the campaign needed to be highly targeted and conversion-optimized from the start.

Results

  • Daily budget of CAD $13 over 6 months (CAD $2,328 total ad spend).
  • Achieved a 4.6% Click-Through Rate (CTR).
  • Generated 21 leads at a Cost Per Lead (CPL) of CAD $68.
  • Converted 9 qualified leads and secured 2 client contracts with a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of CAD $1,164.
  • Acquired clients with a monthly contract value of CAD $1,293.
