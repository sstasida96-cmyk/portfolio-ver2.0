Overview & Challenge

Renobank Inc. operates in the highly competitive Canadian real estate market, connecting buyers, sellers, and developers. The challenge was building a consistent, high-quality lead generation engine for real estate firms while simultaneously closing property sales contracts.

The dual role of Marketing & Sales Manager required balancing strategic campaign management with direct client relationship and broker negotiation — a cross-functional approach that demanded both analytical thinking and strong communication.

Strategy & Approach

  • Designed, built, and launched conversion-optimized landing pages using no-code tools, tailored to specific property types and buyer segments.
  • Developed marketing initiatives targeting real estate buyers and investors through digital channels, capturing qualified leads for real estate firms.
  • Regularly conducted landing page audits and A/B testing to improve conversion rates — optimizing headlines, hero sections, CTAs, and form design.
  • Negotiated directly with brokers and managed client relationships to move prospects through the sales funnel to close.
  • Tracked and reported on key performance metrics to identify optimization opportunities and demonstrate ROI to stakeholders.

Results & Impact

  • Generated a consistent pipeline of 20+ qualified client leads per month for partner real estate firms.
  • Closed a minimum of one property sales contract every month, directly contributing to firm revenue.
  • Improved landing page conversion rates through iterative design and testing, reducing cost per lead over time.
  • Built strong broker relationships that streamlined deal negotiation and accelerated time-to-close.
