Overview

Sales LP for a real estate company targeting new customers in the Sendai area. Campaign ran from Sep–Nov 2025 with a CAD $26/day budget (CAD $1,442 total). The Sendai market turned out to have unique characteristics that required strategic pivoting mid-campaign.

Results

  • Daily budget of CAD $26 from Sep 2025 to Nov 2025, with a total ad spend of CAD $1,442.
  • Achieved a 4.92% Click-Through Rate (CTR).
  • Generated 36 leads at a Cost Per Lead (CPL) of CAD $40.
  • Converted 20 qualified leads and secured 4 client contracts with a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of CAD $360.
  • Closed deals with an average value of approximately CAD $5,172.

Key Insights & Strategic Pivots

  • Discovered that search volume for 'Akiya' (vacant houses) in this area is 3× higher than in other regions — this was a critical market insight not visible from surface-level research.
  • Initial CTR underperformed, so we removed 'how-to' inheritance keywords and changed the campaign goal to increasing click volume.
  • This pivot significantly improved click-through performance and overall campaign efficiency.
