Overview & Challenge

DEA Canadian College needed to scale international student enrollment while maintaining a profitable cost per acquisition. The challenge was standing out in a competitive education market across multiple geographies with varying intent signals.

The goal was clear: generate a sustainable pipeline of highly qualified prospective students — people actively seeking Canadian college programs — and convert them through a streamlined enrollment process.

Strategy & Approach

  • Developed audience segmentation frameworks across Meta and Google, targeting prospective students by geography, interests, and behavioral signals.
  • Built full-funnel ad structures: awareness campaigns via Meta video/image ads, retargeting with testimonial creatives, and Google Search campaigns capturing high-intent queries.
  • Produced ad creative briefs and collaborated on social media content aligned with enrollment seasons.
  • Implemented conversion tracking via Google Tag Manager to attribute leads accurately across all channels.
  • Ran A/B tests on ad copy, landing page headlines, and CTAs to continuously improve conversion rates.

Results & Impact

  • Consistently generated 45+ qualified leads per month — defining qualified as prospects who completed the inquiry form and met program requirements.
  • Achieved a CAD $33 Cost Per Acquisition across combined Meta and Google channels — well below industry benchmarks for education advertising.
  • Closed an average of 12 student enrollments per month, contributing to CAD $18,000 in monthly tuition revenue.
  • Built and maintained partnerships with 10+ recruitment agencies globally, expanding the college's international reach.
  • Optimized web pages and landing experiences to reduce friction and improve lead-to-enrollment conversion rates.
