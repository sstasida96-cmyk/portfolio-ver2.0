Overview

A high-performance sales landing page campaign for a real estate company in the Fukuoka market. Running over 3 months with a CAD $26/day budget (CAD $1,546 total), this campaign became one of the strongest performers in the portfolio in terms of raw lead volume and deal count.

Results

  • Daily budget of CAD $26 over 3 months (CAD $1,546 total ad spend).
  • Achieved an 8.98% Click-Through Rate (CTR).
  • Generated 54 leads at a Cost Per Lead (CPL) of CAD $29.
  • Converted 23 qualified leads and secured 7 client contracts with a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of CAD $221.
  • Closed deals with an average value of approximately CAD $6,897.

Responsibilities

  • Landing Page Strategy & Design
  • No-Code Development (Webflow)
  • Google Ads Management
  • Marketing Analytics & Optimization
  • CRM Setup & Operations
  • Lead Generation & Conversion Optimization
