Mental Health Lab Inc. — New Business Launch & Scale

Led the end-to-end launch and growth of a new mental health business from zero — building a full-funnel marketing engine that generated ¥10M JPY in Year 1, maintained a stable ¥5,000 CPA, and reduced operational workload by 30% through custom AI automation.

Overview & Challenge

Mental Health Lab Inc. was a new venture entering a sensitive, trust-dependent market in Japan. Building awareness, establishing credibility, and converting prospects into paying clients — all while managing a lean team — required an exceptionally efficient and well-structured marketing strategy.

The challenge was threefold: launch a brand from scratch, build a scalable acquisition funnel, and maintain profitability from day one. Additionally, the operational demands of running a new business needed to be offset through automation to prevent team burnout.

Strategy & Approach

  • Defined the brand positioning, messaging framework, and target audience segments to establish credibility in the mental health space.
  • Built a full-funnel marketing strategy: top-of-funnel awareness content, mid-funnel nurture sequences, and bottom-funnel conversion campaigns across digital channels.
  • Designed and launched landing pages optimized for appointment bookings and service inquiries, using A/B testing to drive down CPA.
  • Developed custom AI-powered workflow tools using platforms like ChatGPT and automation builders to handle repetitive tasks — client inquiry routing, report generation, and follow-up sequences.
  • Tracked funnel performance weekly, adjusting spend allocation and creative messaging based on conversion data to keep CPA at or below ¥5,000 JPY.

Results & Impact

  • Successfully launched and scaled the business, generating ¥10,000,000 JPY in revenue within the first year of operations.
  • Maintained a stable Cost Per Acquisition of ¥5,000 JPY throughout the growth phase, ensuring profitability from the outset.
  • Built and deployed custom AI automation tools that reduced team operational workload by 30%, freeing resources for higher-value activities.
  • Established brand presence and trust in a competitive market through consistent content, community engagement, and targeted paid campaigns.
