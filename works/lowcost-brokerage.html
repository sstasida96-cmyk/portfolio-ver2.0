Overview

A short, high-ROI campaign built for a real estate company positioning itself around low brokerage fees. The tight 1-month window required precise targeting and a compelling value proposition to convert on a lean budget of CAD $343.

The campaign achieved a strong 8.29% CTR and closed a deal with approximately 16.6× return on ad spend.

Results

  • Daily budget of CAD $26 over 1 month (CAD $343 total ad spend).
  • Achieved an 8.29% Click-Through Rate (CTR).
  • Generated 4 leads at a Cost Per Lead (CPL) of CAD $84.
  • Converted 3 qualified leads and secured 1 client contract with a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of CAD $343.
  • Closed a deal with an average value of approximately CAD $5,690.

Responsibilities

  • Landing Page Strategy & Design
  • No-Code Development (Webflow)
  • Google Ads Management
  • Marketing Analytics & Optimization
  • CRM Setup & Operations
  • Lead Generation & Conversion Optimization
